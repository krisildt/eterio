04A WORKING METHOD

Keep the whole
in view.

A small set of useful checkpoints. The order flexes with the problem; specialist work is not forced through a generic funnel.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Find the real constraint before choosing a medium.

  2. 02

    Shape

    Make the brief, the system, and the point of view clear.

  3. 03

    Build

    Carry design decisions into a working, testable thing.

  4. 04

    Verify

    Check what people actually encounter, then refine.

A trademark-readiness brief and a digital product do not need the same process. The point is to keep the next decision clear.
