01 FICTIONAL CONCEPT / MEDIA

Nocturne.

A listening space for quieter discovery

Speculative / not client work

This original study is fictional. It does not represent a real client, product, or commercial outcome.

THE BRIEF / IN FICTION

Nocturne is a fictional concept study about attention, listening, and the small rituals around finding a record. It is not a client commission or a commercial product.

Discipline
Media
Status
Speculative concept
Scope
Visual and experience study
01 / MEDIA

The question

Explore a calmer alternative to endless browsing: a small, editorial listening environment where the artwork and the act of choosing share equal weight.

02 / MEDIA

The direction

A restrained tonal palette, a physical-feeling disc, and a narrow amber signal give the idea a visual rhythm without relying on genre clichés.

03 / MEDIA

The system

The concept pairs an imagined listening experience with an identity direction and a short motion language. The image is AI-generated concept artwork for a fictional study, not a real service; its generation record and usage terms still require owner confirmation.
