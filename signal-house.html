02 FICTIONAL CONCEPT / TECHNOLOGY

Signal House.

A clearer home for complex work

Speculative / not client work

This original study is fictional. It does not represent a real client, product, or commercial outcome.

THE BRIEF / IN FICTION

Signal House is a speculative web-application concept. The name, interface language, and scenario are fictional and are shown only to demonstrate an approach.

Discipline
Technology
Status
Speculative concept
Scope
Visual and experience study
01 / TECHNOLOGY

The question

Consider how a dense workspace might help people see what needs attention without turning every task into another notification.

02 / TECHNOLOGY

The direction

The art direction uses strong typographic anchors, quiet surfaces, and a visible system map to make relationships easier to scan.

03 / TECHNOLOGY

The system

The concept combines an information architecture, interaction patterns, and a front-end study. The image is illustrative material for this fictional concept, not a product screen or client system; its source and rights are not recorded.
